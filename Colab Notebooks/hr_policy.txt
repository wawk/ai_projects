# Company HR Policy Handbook

## 1. Leave Policy
All full-time employees are entitled to the following types of leave:
**Anual Leave** - 18 working days per calendar year.
**Sick Leave** - 14 days with medical certificate; 60 days with hospitalization
"""

##2. Remote Work & Hybrid Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Request to work remotely overseas mus be submitted to HR 2 weeks in advance.

##3. Working Hours:

Standard hours: Monday-Friday, 09:00 -18:00 with a 1 hour lunch break. Flexible arrangements can be made with manager.

##4. Fratenization Policy: At this company we encourage social on premise and after hours interaction. We believe this lends itself to our mantra "We are family here"
Social interaction is permitted with management with HR documentation and with customers as well.

##5. Parking: Parking on premise during working hours and days is permitted with an approved parking pass available from Parking Enforcement.
Parking after hours or on days not scheduled as working will be subject to a warning. 3 warnings will result in a fine. More than 7 warnings will result in suspension or termination.

##6. NDA - All employees will sign an NDA within 3 business days of their start of employment. All work produced during work scheduled work hours whether on premise or not 
is considered owned by the company.

##7. Bonuses - Yearly Bonuses will be determined by the yearly review and will be available in the next paycheck following thier approval.

##8. Yearly Company Retreat: The yearly company retreat is required attendance by all employees unless a excusal permit is advaquired from management at least 
2 weeks prior. The company picks up all expenses on transportation to and from the event, as well as lodging, food and amenities.
